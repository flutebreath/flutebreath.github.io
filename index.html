Frequently asked questions

What is Flute Breath Timer?

A free browser-based tool that automatically times how long you sustain a note on the flute. It listens through your microphone and detects when a note starts and stops, so you don't need to press any buttons while you're playing.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs entirely in your browser at flutebreath.github.io — no app store, no download, and no account to create.

Is my microphone audio uploaded or recorded?

No. All audio processing happens locally in your browser using the Web Audio API. Nothing is ever uploaded, recorded to a file, or sent to a server.

Does it work on iPhone and other mobile devices?

Yes. Open the site in a mobile browser like Safari, allow microphone access, and it works like any other page. You can also add it to your home screen for quicker access.

Can I use it for instruments other than flute?

The detection is based on loudness rather than flute tone specifically, so it can time any sustained note — though it's built and named around flute breath practice.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free, with no ads, no account, and no in-app purchases.
