Frequently asked questions

What is Flute Breath Timer?

A free browser-based tool that automatically times how long you sustain a note on the flute. It listens through your microphone and detects when a note starts and stops, so you don't need to press any buttons while you're playing.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs entirely in your browser at flutebreath.github.io — no app store, no download, and no account to create.

Is my microphone audio uploaded or recorded?

By default, no — all audio processing happens locally in your browser using the Web Audio API, and nothing is ever uploaded or sent to a server. There's one optional, off-by-default toggle that records only your current best attempt's clip locally so you can download it; it's gated behind a confirmation dialog, replaces itself automatically when you beat it, and is never uploaded anywhere.

Does it work on iPhone and other mobile devices?

Yes. Open the site in a mobile browser like Safari, allow microphone access, and it works like any other page. You can also add it to your home screen for quicker access.

Can I use it for instruments other than flute?

The detection is based on loudness rather than flute tone specifically, so it can time any sustained note — though it's built and named around flute breath practice.

Can I practice Indian classical swaras (Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni) with this app?

Yes. Set your Sa (tonic) from a note dropdown, pick which swara you're practicing, and the app gives live green/yellow/red pitch feedback while you play, plus how many cents sharp or flat you are. It uses standard 12-tone equal temperament, not full microtonal shruti precision.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free, with no ads, no account, and no in-app purchases.

⚠️ Recording your audio

Turning this on records your microphone audio locally in your browser while you play. Only your current best attempt's clip is kept — it's automatically replaced the moment you beat it — and it never leaves this device or gets uploaded anywhere.

Turning it back off deletes the stored clip immediately.
